# Prompt Engineering Activity 
### The Samal Eco-Tourism Digital Coordination Prompt System 

#### 1. System Prompt Template
Act as a Senior Tourism Development and Community Coordination Officer assigned to a Local Government Unit (LGU) in Samal Island, Davao del Norte, Philippines.

### Context:
Focus strictly on Samal Island eco-tourism systems, including:
- Beach and marine conservation zones
- Local ferry and transport coordination from Davao City to Samal Island
- Small tourism enterprises (homestays, local guides, food vendors)
- Environmental protection policies for coastal barangays

### Constraints:
- Use formal LGU-level communication tone
- Avoid global tourism examples or foreign case studies
- Do not reference international travel systems or non-local destinations
- Focus only on Samal Island and nearby Davao Region infrastructure
- Prioritize sustainability, safety, and community livelihood

### Format:
- Output in Markdown
- Must include section:
  ### Eco-Tourism Operational Actions
- Provide exactly 3 actionable steps:
  - One focused on environment
  - One focused on transport/logistics
  - One focused on community livelihood

### Objective:
Ensure outputs are usable by LGU Technical Working Groups for real-time eco-tourism coordination and policy action.

# AI Study Tools & Platforms Activity 
### Literature Verification Log

#### Topic: Sustainable Fisheries and Coastal Resource Management in Davao Gulf
#### 1. AI-Generated Summary Audit
I prompted an AI research tool to summarize recent studies on fisheries sustainability and coastal management practices in the Davao Gulf region. Below is the verification tracking matrix comparing AI outputs with validated academic and government sources.

| AI-Generated Statement / Citation                                                                                               | Source Vetted Against                                                         | Status              | Human Correction / Empirical Note                                                                                                             |
| :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Fish catch volume in Davao Gulf increased by 40% in 2023 due to improved marine protected area enforcement."                   | Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR) Region XI Annual Report 2023 | ❌ **Hallucination** | No documented 40% increase. BFAR reports indicate fluctuating yields with localized improvements in protected zones but no region-wide surge. |
| "Marine Protected Areas (MPAs) in Davao del Sur have contributed to measurable coral reef recovery and fish biomass stability." | DENR Coastal Resource Management Reports                                      | ✔ **Verified**      | Supported by multiple coastal assessments showing gradual reef recovery in well-managed MPAs.                                                 |
| "Illegal fishing activities have been fully eliminated in monitored zones due to strict enforcement technologies."              | Coastal Law Enforcement Task Force Reports (Davao Gulf)                       | ❌ **Hallucination** | Illegal fishing persists at reduced but significant levels; enforcement has improved but is not fully eliminating violations.                 |
#### 2. Critical Reflection on Tool Limitations

While the AI system was effective in synthesizing multiple environmental and fisheries management reports into a structured summary, several inaccuracies emerged due to overgeneralization of environmental outcomes and inflation of policy effectiveness.
The model tended to interpret localized improvements in Marine Protected Areas as full-scale regional success, ignoring spatial variation across different coastal municipalities. Additionally, enforcement outcomes were overstated, failing to reflect ongoing challenges in illegal fishing activities and limited patrol coverage.
This highlights the importance of human validation when working with environmental governance data, particularly in coastal regions where ecological conditions vary significantly between barangays and enforcement jurisdictions.
